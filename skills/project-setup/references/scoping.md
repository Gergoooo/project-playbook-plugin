## 0. Scope the idea (plan mode)

- Lay out the feature list / rough idea to Claude, ask it to plan.
- Push back on vague answers — for every "what should we use" question, expect a recommendation with tradeoffs, not just a menu. Debate the ones that matter (e.g. framework choice) before locking in.
- Expect Claude to ask clarifying questions on anything it can't infer: hosting, AI provider, data sources, distribution target. Answer these deliberately — they shape everything downstream.
- Decide the **license** (`references/licensing.md`): ask, recommend, record in DECISIONS.
- Explicitly state constraints that matter to you: budget (free/paid), privacy/legal posture, solo vs team, timeline pressure. These change the right answer more than the tech itself does.
